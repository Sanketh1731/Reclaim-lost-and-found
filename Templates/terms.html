1. Community Code of Conduct

ReClaim is built on trust, honesty, and goodwill. Users agree to:

  • Report genuine lost and found belongings only.
  • Verify item ownership before handing over valuable property.
  • Refrain from posting spam, fraudulent claims, or abusive language.
  • Mark returned items promptly to keep listings current and earn reputation points.

2. Safe Exchange Best Practices

When meeting to return or claim an item, we recommend arranging exchanges in well-lit, public locations such as campus security offices, library front desks, or public reception areas.
