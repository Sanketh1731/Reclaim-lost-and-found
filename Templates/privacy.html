Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ current_year }}

1. Information We Collect

ReClaim collects information you provide directly when registering an account, submitting a lost item report, or submitting a found item report. This includes your name, email address, contact information, item details, location descriptions, and optional item photographs.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to facilitate the matching and recovery of lost belongings within your community or campus. Specifically:

  • To match lost and found reports using our smart matching engine.
  • To display report descriptions and contact info to verified users.
  • To send email or in-app notifications when potential matches are identified.
  • To prevent fraud and maintain community reputation scores.

3. Image & Media Handling

Uploaded photos are stored securely on our servers and displayed only with their associated report. We recommend avoiding images that contain sensitive personal identification documents (such as government IDs or credit card numbers) unless key sensitive numbers are obscured.

4. Account & Data Control

You can edit or delete your reported items at any time through your personal Dashboard. Deleting a report immediately removes its data and attached images from our active databases.
